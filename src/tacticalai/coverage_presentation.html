What changed

The same YOLO26s detections are used on both sides: confidence 0.10 and image size 1280. TrackTrack gates changed from high/low/new = 0.60/0.25/0.70 to 0.25/0.10/0.25. Confirmation remains 3 frames; track-aware overlap suppression, ReID yolo26n-cls, GMC sparseOptFlow and buffer 30 stay fixed. Both controls were replayed locally; the control reproduced the original track observation counts.

Automatic court geometry removes candidates estimated outside the court with little body/court overlap. A 3 m safety margin and uncertain/clipped cases are retained. This is deliberately conservative: approximate cameras and hidden feet can retain spectators or misclassify boundary cases. It is not a player/referee classifier.

Every retained detection is available even while an ID is pending. YOLO26s-seg masks were recomputed independently of track confirmation. A missing mask leaves the automatic label visible; no body shape or hidden player is invented.

Measured sample coverage · evaluation only

Inspected visible-head points on eight source frames measure whether distinct people are represented. Each box can match one point. These human inspection points never enter inference, filtering or overlays. This is a small development sample, not box AP, precision, verified identity accuracy or a held-out benchmark.
