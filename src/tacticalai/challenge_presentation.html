About this preview & exports

Automatic court calibration uses the KaliCalib challenge checkpoint with per-frame camera refinement. Player observations reuse YOLO26s + TrackTrack with ReID. Positions use bounding-box bottom centres as approximate floor contact; colors represent tracker IDs, not teams or jersey identities.

Player/referee roles are manually reviewed labels for these saved runs. Tracking errors and missed players remain. When the camera estimate is rejected, player boxes remain visible and the court map shows no projected dots. No hidden players or off-screen trajectories are inferred. This is offline rendered playback at source FPS; accuracy and live processing speed are not established. Audio is omitted.
