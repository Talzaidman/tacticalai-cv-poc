Validation & remaining gaps

No accuracy evaluation loaded. Prediction coverage does not establish correct classification.

Two uniform groups are assumed, with no assigned jersey colors or fixed number of players. A / B are local to each clip and do not identify a roster. Roles use a generic pretrained CLIP model with configurable prompts and abstention thresholds; similarity scores are not probabilities.

Classification, identity linking and smoothing use the complete clip, including future frames. Upstream parameters and the choice of role model, prompts and crop preprocessing were developed using these clips. Evaluation is a development spot check. No manual role/team labels or identity corrections enter inference. Court size assumes 28 × 15 m and bounding-box bottoms approximate floor contact. Ball, jersey numbers, hidden trajectories and tactics remain unimplemented.
