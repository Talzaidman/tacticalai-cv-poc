Method, limitations and provenance

The original winning-channel centroid extraction and 35-pixel RANSAC threshold run at 960×540. Camera refinement uses OpenCV's constrained pinhole fit and the upstream floor-span check. Both supplied pretrained checkpoints use identical inputs and settings.

Two explicit adaptations: failed estimation returns unknown rather than the upstream fixed mean-camera fallback; refinement and its image probes consistently use 960×540 before scaling to source pixels. Elevated basket channels are excluded. No manual coordinates enter detection, RANSAC or refinement.

Manual discrepancies allow the best court-axis reflection separately at each checkpoint. They are optimistic agreement diagnostics against draft clicks, not ground-truth accuracy or player-position error. Clip 2 has a manual reference only at frame 0. Frames 25 onward show no manual overlay there. Camera acceptance and inlier residuals do not prove correct geometry. No thresholds were tuned against these manual clicks.
