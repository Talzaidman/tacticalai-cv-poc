Scores, rejected pairs and limitations

Scores are not probabilities. The appearance model is CLIP-ReIdent. The comparison method is shown with each score; whole-ID connections use one-to-one assignment. The gallery aggregation is our baseline extension, not a reproduced challenge pipeline. Thresholds were chosen on development observations.

Whole-track merges are rejected when track lifetimes overlap. Existing switches inside an ID are not repaired in this experiment. A lower count alone does not establish correct identity matching. No ground-truth recovery accuracy has been measured.
