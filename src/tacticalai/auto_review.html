What these results do and do not establish

Geometry checks measure consistency among model predictions. They do not establish correct court alignment. Both models may confidently predict incorrect locations. KaliCalib predicts a grid of floor correspondences; these are not all physical line intersections. Its elevated basket channels are excluded. The YOLO landmark mapping is unverified, so its raw outputs are not used for homography.

* Manual discrepancy compares independent automatic predictions with existing draft manual clicks, allowing the four court-axis reflections separately at each checkpoint because court orientation has not been resolved. This is an optimistic diagnostic, not ground-truth accuracy, not player-position error, and not a way to initialize the automatic method. Clip 1 has seven checkpoints; clip 2 has one.

Temporal mode re-detects every five frames or on tracking loss, with at most four successive flow frames. Moving-person boxes and the bottom score region are excluded from feature selection. Cut detection is a coarse experimental safeguard. Rejected calibration means unknown positions, with no manual fallback. The automatic map uses the model's court axes, which can differ from the manual map.

The map reuses YOLO26s / TrackTrack + ReID observations. Clip 1 uses existing manual player/referee role labels; clip 2 shows people with unverified roles. Colors identify tracker IDs, not teams. Missing players and off-screen locations are not inferred. These are offline rendered videos at source FPS, not a live-throughput demonstration.
